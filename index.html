THE FINAL SHIFT · 1980s → 2024–26

DESIGN KEPT CHANGING. SO DID THE JOB DESCRIPTION.

As design expanded from specialist production into digital products, services, systems and AI-supported workflows, the skills expected of emerging designers and the ways employers assessed them also changed. These shifts reshaped the journey from education into work.

INTERPRETATION Roles, tools, employers and hiring methods moved together, but not at the same pace, and not the same way in every place. Some programmes adapted quickly; others worked under different constraints. This is a picture of variation, not a verdict that education failed.

Research boundary. Secondary research only: published, linked sources. Eras are narrative groupings we chose, not official periods (the late-2010s era overlaps 2015 on purpose). India and global entries were researched separately. Where we found no dated source, the line is marked interpretation.
